# Lost Pet Finder — Class Project Plan

## Audience
Pet owners who just lost their pet and need help fast (within the first hour).

## Problem It Solves
They don't know the best places nearby to search for their missing pet.

## Pages

### 1. Home Page
- Two clear buttons:
  - **I lost a pet**
  - **I spotted a pet**

### 2. Lost Pet Form
For the pet owner to fill out. Includes:
- Photo of pet
- Pet color
- Pet gender
- Breed
- Last known location

### 3. Report a Sighting Form
For street walkers who spotted a stray. Features:
- Quick to fill out
- No account needed

### 4. Sightings Results
Shows the pet owner where nearby people have spotted a matching pet, so they know the best places to search.

---

## Key Design Rules
- Fast and simple — the owner is panicking
- No sign-up required for reporting a sighting
- Focused on one problem: where to search
