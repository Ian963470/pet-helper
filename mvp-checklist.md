# MVP Ready Check — Lesson 7

## Home Page
- [ ] Site name is visible
- [ ] One sentence explaining what the site does
- [ ] "I lost a pet" button works and goes to the Lost Pet Form
- [ ] "I spotted a pet" button works and goes to the Report a Sighting Form

## Lost Pet Form
- [ ] Fields for color, size, gender, street, city, and contact info
- [ ] Optional photo field is available
- [ ] Submit button works and takes owner to the Sightings Results page

## Report a Sighting Form
- [ ] Fields for color, size, gender, street, city, and phone number
- [ ] Submit button works
- [ ] If a match is found, stranger sees matching lost pet reports
- [ ] If no match, a "No matches yet, but your report was saved" message appears

## Sightings Results Page
- [ ] Shows a list of sightings matching the lost pet's color, size, and gender
- [ ] Each card shows street, city, pet description, stranger's phone number, and date/time
- [ ] Most recent sightings appear at the top
- [ ] Tip is shown: "Found your pet? Next time, put a tag with your phone number on their collar."
