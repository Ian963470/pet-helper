🆘 What to do when your pet goes missing

Act fast. The first hour is the most important. Do not wait.
  1. Search your immediate street first — pets rarely go far in the first hour.
  2. Ask neighbors to check their gardens, sheds, and garages.
  3. Put a familiar-smelling item like a blanket outside your front door — it may help your pet find its way home.
  4. Call local vets and animal shelters to report your pet missing.
  5. Post on local community social media groups with a photo and your phone number.
  6. Check back on Lost Pet Finder regularly — new sightings are added by people in your area.
  7. Found your pet? Next time, put a tag with your phone number on their collar.

Fast facts about lost pets

  • 🐕 About 10 million pets are lost in the United States every year.
  • 🐈 Only about 2% of lost cats that enter shelters are reunited with their owners — most are found by neighbors.
  • 📍 The most common reason pets go missing is an open gate or door.
  • 🌙 Pets are more likely to hide at night — search near bushes, under cars, and in quiet spots.
  • 🔊 Calling your pet's name loudly can sometimes attract other animals — try shaking a treat bag instead.
  • 📋 Microchipped pets are up to 20 times more likely to be returned to their owner than non-microchipped pets.
